How to use it, in plain English. No experience needed.

What this app is

It keeps track of three things for you:

1. Your product, how many bottles and kits you have, and where they are.

2. Your shows, where you sell.

3. Your money, what came in each day and how you got paid.

You count your product in the morning, count again at night, and punch in the money you took. The app does all the math.

One idea to understand first: the Garage

The app thinks of your stock as living in one of two places:

🏠 The Garage is everything sitting at home. The STOCK tab shows your garage.

🎪 A Show is whatever you packed and took with you to sell.

When you start a show, product moves from the garage to the show. When the show ends, whatever did not sell moves back to the garage. Product is never lost, it just changes location.

First time setup

  1. Open the app link in your phone's browser.
  2. Add it to your home screen so it works like a regular app. iPhone: tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: tap the three dots menu, then "Add to Home screen".
  3. Go to ⚙️ SETUP and sign in. This backs up your data to the cloud and keeps every device you use showing the same thing.
  4. Go to 📦 STOCK and enter how many of each product you have at home. Now the app knows your garage.

🎪 How to start a show

There are two parts. You create the show ahead of time, then you start it the day you pack up.

Part 1, create it (any time before the show):

  1. Go to 🎪 SHOWS and tap + New Show.
  2. Fill in the name, the town, the start date, how many days it runs, the booth cost, and one-way miles.
  3. Tap Create Show. It now sits in your list as a planned show.

Part 2, start it (when you pack your truck):

  1. Tap the show in your 🎪 SHOWS list to open it.
  2. Tap 🎒 Start Show / Pack Inventory.
  3. Enter how many of each product you are bringing. It cannot be more than what is in your garage.
  4. Tap ✅ Start Show. Done. That stock moved out of your garage and into the show, and your day 1 morning count is already filled in for you.

During the show, every day

  1. Morning: make sure your morning count is right. Day 1 is automatic from packing.
  2. All day: just sell. You do not need the app while selling.
  3. Night: count what is LEFT on the table and enter it as the evening count. The app figures out what you sold. Morning minus evening equals sold.
  4. Enter the money you took, split by how you got it: Cash, Square, Venmo, and so on.
  5. Enter what you spent that day: gas, food, parking, hotel.
💡 The counts are the whole game. Count careful in the morning and careful at night, and every number in the app will be right.

🔄 How to move stock from one show to another

Say you have product parked in one show and you need it at a different show, or you grabbed 10 more bottles from home. Do NOT just change the counts by hand. Use Transfer, it keeps everything honest.

  1. Open either show and tap the blue 🔄 Transfer Stock (garage or another show) button. You can also get there from Master Inventory on HOME with 🔄 Transfer Garage ⇄ Show.
  2. Pick the product and how many.
  3. Pick From and To. From can be the garage or any show, and To can be the garage or any show.
  4. Tap ✅ Transfer. That is it.

A transfer is not a sale. It does not touch your money, your sold counts, or anybody's commission. It just moves product from one spot to another, like carrying a box across the room.

Made a mistake? Every transfer is saved in a list and can be undone.

🏁 Ending a show

  1. Open the show and tap 🏁 End Show & Return Inventory.
  2. Confirm. Whatever did not sell goes back to your garage automatically, and the show moves to your Completed list with all its numbers saved.

Common questions

My phone shows different stuff than the other guy's phone. Which one is right?

Two things cause this, and both are easy to fix.

1. An old app icon. Scroll to the bottom of the HOME screen. It should say v7.8 or newer. If it shows a lower number, your home screen icon is pointing at an old copy. Delete the icon, open the app link fresh in your browser, and add it to your home screen again.

2. Not signed in. The app saves to YOUR phone. Two phones only show the same data when both are signed in to the same account under ⚙️ SETUP. If it says "Saved on this device" at the bottom of SETUP, you are not synced.

I entered a wrong number. Am I stuck with it?

No. Open the show, open that day, fix the number. The app recalculates everything.

Will I lose my data if I close the app?

No. It saves automatically every time you touch anything. Sign in under ⚙️ SETUP and it is also backed up to the cloud.

Do I need internet at the show?

No. The app works offline. It syncs up the next time you have signal.

The golden rules

1. Count in the morning. Count at night.
2. Log every dollar the day you take it.
3. Moving product? Use 🔄 Transfer, never edit counts by hand.
4. Sign in under ⚙️ SETUP so your data is backed up and synced.
